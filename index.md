# Privacy Policy for Carlink-Native

**Last updated:** March 13, 2026

## Overview
Carlink-Native ("the App") enables connectivity between an Android Automotive OS (AAOS) device and a compatible wireless Android Auto dongle.

The App functions as a local connectivity and compatibility layer to allow Android Auto to operate wirelessly on supported hardware.

The developer of this App does **not collect, store, or share personal information** from users.

---

## Microphone Permission
The App requests the permission:

`android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`

This permission is required so that Android Auto voice features can function properly when the App is active. These features may include:

- Voice commands  
- Hands-free calling  
- Voice assistant interactions  
- Messaging dictation  

Audio captured through the microphone:

- Is used **only for real-time voice interaction**
- Is **not recorded, stored, or transmitted by this App**
- Is handled by **Android system services or Android Auto components**

The App itself does **not process or retain microphone recordings**.

---

## Data Collection
The App **does not collect personal data**.

Specifically, the App does not collect:

- Audio recordings  
- Location information  
- Contacts  
- Messages  
- User accounts  
- Device identifiers  
- Analytics or usage tracking data  

All functionality occurs locally on the device.

---

## Third-Party Services
The App interacts with platform services provided by:

- Google (Android Automotive OS and Android Auto)

These services may process data as described in their own privacy policies.

---

## Data Sharing
The App **does not sell, rent, or share user data** with any third parties.

---

## Data Storage
The App does not store personal data on external servers.

Any temporary data used for connectivity or device pairing remains **local to the device** and is managed by Android system services.

---

## Security
Because the App does not collect or transmit personal data, no personal information is retained by the developer.

---

## Children's Privacy
This App is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
